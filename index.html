Laasya,

I've rewritten this about nine times, because words keep feeling too small for what I'm trying to say.

Here's the honest version: you make everything softer. Mornings, bad days, the long boring stretches in between. You have this way of listening like whatever I'm saying actually matters — and of laughing like the whole world is in on the joke.

I don't want a perfect story. I want the ordinary one. Grocery runs and inside jokes and arguing over what to watch. I want the version where it's you, on the plain days, for a very long time.

So I built a whole website. Because apparently I'm dramatic when it comes to you.

yours, always 💗
